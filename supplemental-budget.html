Supplemental Budget

Use this when the SK needs to move funds (reversion or realignment) or use new funds during the year. Make the papers in this order.

No spending yet. Nothing can be paid from the supplemental budget until the Sangguniang Bayan has reviewed it. The total budget should not go up unless there are new funds.

  1. Resolution: reversion of funds

    Free up the money first. Say where it comes from and why it is not needed there.

  2. Treasurer certification

    The Treasurer certifies that the funds are really available and not committed.

  3. SBYIP

    List the new or changed program, project or activity (PPA).

  4. PAD

    Project Activity Design of the new activity, with its detailed budget.

  5. Resolution approving the SBYIP

    Approve the SBYIP in an SK session.

  6. Supplemental Budget

    Put the source of funds (Part I) and the new appropriation (Part II).

  7. Resolution approving the Supplemental Budget

    Approve the supplemental budget in an SK session.

  8. Put the packet together and send to the SB

    Cover page on top, then the letter, and all the papers above. Submit through the Municipal Budget Officer.
