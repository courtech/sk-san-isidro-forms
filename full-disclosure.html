Full Disclosure

The SK posts these on the barangay Full Disclosure Policy (FDP) board, in compliance with DILG MC 2023-068.

See a sample online FDP board: SK Barangay San Miguel.

  1. CBYDP

    Post once approved. It covers 3 years. Get the template.

  2. ABYIP

    Post every year once approved. Get the template.

  3. SK Annual Budget

    Post every year once approved. Get the template.

  4. Register of Cash in Bank (RCB)

    The SK Treasurer records every deposit and check here (official COA format, Annex 6). Post a copy every quarter. Ask LandBank for the bank statement to check the balance.

    Prepared by the SK Treasurer.

  5. Monthly Itemized List of Purchase Requests

    List all purchase requests (PRs) of the month. Post the 3 lists of the quarter. Page 2 is the blank PR form from the same official template.

    Prepared by the SK Treasurer, noted by the SK Chairperson.
