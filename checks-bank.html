Checks & Bank

Forms for the SK's LandBank account.

  1. SKCC – for encashing checks

    List the checks issued with their DV numbers ready. The SK Chairperson signs. Give it to LandBank (required under COA Circular 2019-001).

  2. LandBank Customer Request Form (CRF)

    LandBank's own form for account requests (bank statement, check status, and others).

  3. Letter requesting a bank statement

    Needed for the quarterly Registry of Cash in Bank (RCB) and for liquidation.
