Fidelity Bond

The SK Chairperson and SK Treasurer must be bonded with the Bureau of the Treasury (BTr) every year. Follow these steps for the renewal.

Renew before your bond expires (check the date on last year's Confirmation Letter). If it already expired, you file as New/Expired. The checklist in Step 1 lists the extra papers for that.

  1. Check what you need

    Print the checklist and tick each item as you finish it.

  2. Fill out the FBAF

    One form for the SK Chairperson and one for the SK Treasurer. Attach a passport-size photo taken within the last 3 months. Have item 16 notarized.

    Signed by the officer and the Punong Barangay.

  3. Make the List of Bonded Officers

    Put the SK Chairperson and SK Treasurer. You can leave the amount columns blank if BTr has not given them yet.

    Prepared by the Treasurer, reviewed by the Secretary, approved by the Chairperson.

  4. Attach your approved SK Annual Budget

    Scan your signed approved SK Annual Budget and the resolution approving it. No form to download here. Use your own approved copy.

  5. Make the two inventory reports

    Property & equipment (RIPPE/RIDPE) and supplies & materials (RIPSM/RIDSM). If the SK has none, write that in the table and still sign.

  6. Attach last year's Confirmation Letter

    Scan the Confirmation Letter BTr gave you last year.

  7. Submit to BTr

    Scan everything to PDF. Upload it in the BTr Online Fidelity Bonding System, or email it if your BTr office asked for email.

  8. Pay the premium

    BTr will tell you the amount. Pay by SK check (with a Disbursement Voucher). Keep the new Confirmation Letter for next year.
