CBYDP (3-Year Plan)

The Comprehensive Barangay Youth Development Plan covers 3 years (2027–2029). It is the basis of every ABYIP.

  1. Hold a KK Assembly

    Present the plan to the Katipunan ng Kabataan and have attendees sign.

  2. Make the CBYDP

    List the youth concerns, objectives and PPAs for each center of participation.

  3. Resolution approving the CBYDP

    Approve the CBYDP in an SK session, with the KK's concurrence.

  4. Next: make the ABYIP

    Once approved, go to ABYIP & SK Annual Budget.
