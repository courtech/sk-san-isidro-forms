Liquidation & Reports

The official COA forms for paying, liquidating and reporting the SK fund. All are from the COA Handbook on the Financial Transactions of the SK.

Keep the Treasurer's RCB updated. The bank reconciliation and the quarterly report are both made from the RCB (on the Full Disclosure page).

  1. Buying supplies, equipment or services: PR, PO, IAR

    Every purchase needs all three. First the Purchase Request (PR), approved by the SK Chairperson (page 2 of the Monthly PR list). Then the Purchase Order (PO), signed by the supplier and the SK Chairperson. Send a copy of the PO to the COA Auditor within 5 days. When the items arrive, make the Inspection and Acceptance Report (IAR), with the supplier's delivery receipt.

    Buy supplies for up to 3 months only. The IAR is signed by the Head of the Inspection and Appraisal Committee and the SK Treasurer.

    Paying for a service (referees, sound system, catering, repair labor): you still need the PR and PO, but no IAR. Attach the supplier's official receipt and the agreement or quotation instead.

  2. Disbursement Voucher (DV)

    Every payment from the SK fund needs a DV with complete supporting documents. Box A is signed by the Budget Monitoring Officer, Box B by the SK Treasurer, Box C by the SK Chairperson. Box D is signed by the payee when the check is received.

    For the check itself, see the SKCC on Checks & Bank.

  3. Liquidation Report (LR)

    Liquidate a cash advance as soon as its purpose is done. For local travel, liquidate within 30 days after you return. Attach the receipts and other supporting documents.

    Box A: the SK official who got the cash advance. Box B: SK Chairperson. Box C: SK Treasurer.

  4. Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)

    Every month, compare the RCB with the LandBank bank statement. Submit to the COA Auditor on or before the 20th day of the next month.

    Prepared by the SK Treasurer, approved by the SK Chairperson.

  5. Quarterly Statement of Receipts and Payments (QSRP)

    Every quarter, total the receipts and payments from the RCB. Submit to the SK, the Sangguniang Bayan and the COA Auditor within 20 days after the quarter ends.

    Prepared by the SK Treasurer, approved by the SK Chairperson.
