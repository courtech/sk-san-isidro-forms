KK Profiling

Make a list of all youth ages 15 to 30 in the barangay, including the SK officials. Follow DILG MC 2022-033; the forms below are its Annexes 1 to 4.

Keep the answers private. Under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), the KK data is for the National Youth Commission's use only. Do not post the list or share it in group chats.

  1. Letter to the Punong Barangay

    The SK Chairperson, through the SK Secretary, informs the Punong Barangay about the profiling and asks for support and an announcement.

    Annex 1. The full DILG memo is in the last step.

  2. Announce the profiling

    Give out information about the profiling. The SK Secretary posts the announcement in 3 conspicuous places in the barangay and/or on social media, until data gathering ends. The SK officials may split into groups by area.

  3. Interview each youth

    Give the youth a copy of the Informed Consent and the KK Survey Questionnaire. Help them fill out the questionnaire, then ask them to sign both.

    Annexes 2 and 3. Print one set per youth.

  4. Put everyone in the KK Youth Profile

    The SK Secretary copies the answers from all questionnaires into the KK Youth Profile. Use the Excel file so the list is easy to update.

    Annex 4.

  5. Submit to the Local Youth Development Office (LYDO)

    The SK Chairperson gives a copy of the KK Youth Profile to the Local Youth Development Office (LYDO).

    To update: add new members the same way. Remove members who are over 30, and give the LYDO the list of overage members.
