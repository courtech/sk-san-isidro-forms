ABYIP & SK Annual Budget

Make these every year for the coming year, in this order. Use the same amounts in the ABYIP and the budget.

You need an approved CBYDP first. Your ABYIP programs come from it. Honoraria may not go above 25% of the SK fund.

  1. ABYIP

    List your programs, projects and activities (PPAs) for the year under each center of participation.

    Prepared by the Treasurer, approved by the Chairperson.

  2. Resolution approving the ABYIP

    Approve the ABYIP in an SK session.

    Certified by the Secretary, signed by the Kagawads, approved by the Chairperson.

  3. PAD for each project

    Make a Project Activity Design for each program in the ABYIP, with its detailed budget.

  4. SK Annual Budget

    Put the ABYIP amounts into the budget with their account codes (PS, MOOE, CO).

  5. Plantilla of SK Officials

    Honoraria of the Kagawads, Secretary and Treasurer.

  6. Resolution approving the SK Annual Budget

    Approve the budget in an SK session.

  7. Send to the Sangguniang Bayan

    Submit the ABYIP, budget, plantilla and resolutions to the SB for review, through the Municipal Budget Officer.
