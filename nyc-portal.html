NYC SK Web Portal

Every SK must have an account in the National Youth Commission's SK Web Portal and encode its Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) report there. Based on DILG MC 2024-061 and the NYC SK Advisories below.

Encode online only. The NYC no longer accepts M&E reports by email, Word or Excel. Only entries encoded in the portal are counted in the National Monitoring Report.

  1. Register an account

    Go to nyc-sk.com and click Register. Pick your location (region, province, municipality, barangay) and your designation (SK), then set a username and password.
    Upload: your Oath of Office as a PDF, 2 MB or less. Other formats or bigger files are rejected.

    Password: at least 8 characters, with 1 capital letter, 1 number and 1 symbol (e.g. Youth2025!). One account per person, with an email not used before. Contact number starts with 9, without the 0. Type N/A in fields that don't apply.

  2. Take the online courses

    The portal's Learning Management System (LMS) has free video courses on planning, budgeting, procurement, monitoring and evaluation. The NYC encourages SK officials to finish them.

  3. Encode the M&E report (M&E Form 1)

    The SK Secretary encodes every program, project and activity (PPA) of the ABYIP, plus the General Administration Program (GAP), in the portal. Entries must match the approved ABYIP, SK budget and the activities actually done.
    When: the portal is open from April until the last day of February of the next year. After the cut-off, late entries are kept for records only and are not counted in the National Monitoring Report.

    Keep the approved ABYIP, DVs, liquidation reports and activity reports with photos at hand while encoding. If someone needs a printed copy, use the portal's Generate Report button.

  4. Stay in good standing

    The LYDO issues the Certificate of Good Standing that SK officials need for the NSTP exemption, excuse from classes and SK Official Eligibility. The LYDO checks for: minutes and attendance of SK meetings; a KK assembly every semester (activity report with photos); the CBYDP and the ABYIP and annual budget, each with its SK resolution and transmittal letter to the LYDO; an updated Full Disclosure board; and an active SK Web Portal account. The LYDO acts on complete requests within 5 working days.

    Templates: CBYDP, ABYIP & Annual Budget, KK Profiling.

  5. Need help?

    Email the NYC SK Web Portal team at skwebportal@nyc.gov.ph, with a copy to the NYC Area Office for your area, or ask the LYDO.
